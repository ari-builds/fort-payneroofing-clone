Residential

Shingle Roofing, Done Right

Full tear-off and re-roof with new underlayment, flashing, and architectural asphalt shingles.

Whether it's a full tear-off or a targeted replacement, shingle roofing is the job we run most often, and the one where the small details (underlayment, flashing, nail pattern) decide whether it lasts 10 years or 30.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish

01

Inspection & Estimate

We climb up, check the decking, and give you a straight assessment and a free quote.

02

Material & Color

Pick your shingle line and color; we order what's in stock locally when we can.

03

Tear-Off & Install

Full tear-off to the deck, new underlayment and flashing, then a clean shingle install.

04

Cleanup & Walkthrough

Magnetic sweep for nails, haul-off, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.

What's included

Every job includes

  • Full tear-off to bare decking
  • New synthetic underlayment
  • New flashing at valleys & penetrations
  • Ridge vent installed or replaced
  • Magnetic nail sweep of the yard
  • Manufacturer-grade architectural shingles

Roof Repairs That Actually Fix It

Leaks, wind-damaged and missing shingles, and failed flashing. Found, fixed, and sealed for good.

Not every problem needs a new roof. Most leaks trace back to one bad flashing detail, a cracked pipe boot, or a run of wind-lifted shingles. We find the actual cause before we patch anything.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish

01

Find the Leak

We trace water stains back to the real entry point, not just the nearest shingle.

02

Diagnose & Quote

You get a clear explanation of what's wrong and a price before any work starts.

03

Repair

Shingles, flashing, boots, or decking replaced as needed, matched to your existing roof.

04

Verify

We check the fix holds, and walk you through exactly what we did.

What's included

Every job includes

  • Leak tracing & diagnosis
  • Shingle & flashing replacement
  • Pipe boot & vent repair
  • Valley & chimney flashing
  • Storm & wind-damage repair
  • Written scope before work starts

Metal Roofing Built to Outlast the House

Standing-seam and exposed-fastener metal systems, installed to outlast the house.

Metal roofing costs more up front than shingles and outlasts them by decades. We install both standing-seam and exposed-fastener systems to spec: proper panel layout, trim, and fastening from day one.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish

01

System Selection

Standing-seam or exposed-fastener, panel style and color, based on your roof and budget.

02

Measure & Order

We measure the roof and order panels cut to spec.

03

Install

Underlayment, panels, trim, and flashing installed in the correct sequence for a weathertight roof.

04

Final Check

Every seam and fastener checked before we call the job complete.

What's included

Every job includes

  • Standing-seam or exposed-fastener panels
  • New underlayment & clips
  • Ridge, eave & rake trim
  • Proper fastening pattern
  • Flashing at all penetrations
  • Decades-long expected lifespan
